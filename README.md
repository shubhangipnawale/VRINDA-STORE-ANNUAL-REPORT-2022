**🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2022**
 Excel Dashboard Project | Data Analysis & Visualization

📌**Project Overview**
This project presents an interactive Annual Sales Dashboard for Vrinda Store (2022) built entirely in Microsoft Excel.
The dashboard provides deep insights into sales performance, customer behavior, order status, channels, demographics, 
and regional trends, helping stakeholders make data-driven business decisions.

🎯**Objective**
To analyze Vrinda Store’s 2022 sales data and:
-Identify key customer segments
-Understand top-performing states and sales channels
-Track monthly sales and order trends
-Provide actionable insights to improve overall sales performance

🛠️**Tools & Techniques Used**
-Microsoft Excel
-Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
-Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
-Slicers for interactivity
-Advanced Excel formulas
-Data Visualization
-Column Charts
-Line Charts
-Pie Charts
-Bar Charts

📊**Dashboard Highlights**
The dashboard includes the following key sections:
🔹**Sales vs Orders (Monthly Trend)**
-Tracks total sales amount and number of orders from January to December
-Helps identify peak and low-performing months
🔹 **Sales by Gender**
 -Women customers contribute ~64% of total sales
 -Men contribute ~36%
🔹 **Order Status Analysis**
-92% orders successfully delivered
-Minimal cancellations, returns, and refunds
🔹**Top 5 States by Sales**
-Maharashtra
-Karnataka
-Uttar Pradesh
-Tamil Nadu
-Telangana
🔹**Orders by Age Group & Gender**
-Adult age group (30–49 years) is the highest contributing segment
-Women dominate across all age groups
🔹**Sales Channels Performance**
-Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra contribute ~80% of total orders
-Other channels have smaller contributions
🔹**Interactive Filters**
-Month
-Category (Blouse, Bottom, Ethnic Dress, Kurta)
-Channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Ajio, etc.)

📈 **Key Insights**
-👩 Women are more likely to purchase compared to men (~65%)
-📍 Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 revenue-generating states
-👥 Adult customers (30–49 yrs) are the largest customer segment
-🛒 Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the most effective sales channels
✅ Final Business Recommendation
🎯 To improve Vrinda Store sales:
-Target women customers aged 30–49 years living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh by running ads, offers, and discount coupons on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
